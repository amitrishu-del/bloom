Privacy Policy

Bloom for iPhone · Effective 30 September 2026

Bloom does not collect any personal data.

Bloom is a focus timer designed to work entirely on your iPhone. This policy explains what that means.

What Bloom collects

Nothing. Bloom has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not connect to any server, and it does not include any third-party code that does.

What stays on your iPhone

So it can carry on where you left off, Bloom remembers a few things on your device:

  • your session lengths, chosen sound, volume and light or dark setting;
  • the session in progress, if any;
  • how many sessions you have finished today.

These are stored only in Bloom's own storage on your iPhone. They are never sent anywhere, and deleting Bloom deletes them.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Bloom schedules them on your iPhone to tell you when a session ends. They are created and delivered entirely on your device. You can turn them off at any time in Settings › Notifications › Bloom.

Children

Bloom collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
