Privacy Policy

Bloom for iPhone · Effective 1 November 2026

Bloom does not collect any personal data.

Bloom is a focus timer designed to work entirely on your iPhone. This policy explains what that means.

What Bloom collects

Nothing. Bloom has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not connect to any server, and it does not include any third-party code that does.

What stays on your iPhone

So it can carry on where you left off, Bloom remembers a few things on your device:

  • your session lengths, chosen sound, volume and light or dark setting;
  • the session in progress, if any;
  • a history of the focus sessions you've finished (when each one ended and how long it was), which Bloom uses to draw your garden and work out your streaks.

These are stored only in Bloom's own storage on your iPhone. They are never sent anywhere, and deleting Bloom deletes them.

Apple Health

If you turn on "Save to Apple Health" for breathing, Bloom saves each breathing session to the Health app on your iPhone as Mindful Minutes (when it started and ended). Bloom only adds these entries; it never reads anything from Health, and never sends health information anywhere. You can turn this off in Bloom, or remove Bloom's access in the Health app, at any time.

Purchases

Bloom Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; Bloom never sees your payment details or Apple Account. Bloom only asks the App Store whether Pro is unlocked on your account.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Bloom schedules them on your iPhone to tell you when a session ends. They are created and delivered entirely on your device. You can turn them off at any time in Settings › Notifications › Bloom.

Children

Bloom collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
