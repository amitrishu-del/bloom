Your garden

Guided breathing for your breaks: Calm, Box or 4-7-8. The lotus closes as you breathe in and blooms as you breathe out, with a soft tone or a gentle voice to follow with your eyes closed. Can save Mindful Minutes to Apple Health.

Privacy

Bloom does not collect any personal data.

No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your settings and your garden stay on your iPhone, and deleting Bloom deletes them. If you turn on Apple Health, breathing sessions are saved to the Health app on your phone, and nowhere else.

I didn't hear anything when a session ended.

Check that notifications are allowed in Settings › Notifications › Bloom, and that your ringer is on.

Can I change how long sessions last?

Yes. Tap Settings in the app: focus runs from 1 to 90 minutes, breaks from 1 to 40. A long break comes after every fourth focus session.

Where did my lily pads go?

The pond shows today's sessions and starts fresh each morning. Every finished session is kept in your garden: tap the flower button to the left of Start.

How do I get Bloom Pro back on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the garden, tap See Bloom Pro, then Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In your iPhone's Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › Bloom. Pro stays unlocked until the end of the period you've paid for. The lifetime purchase is paid once and never renews.

Can the breathing voice sound more natural?

Yes. Bloom uses the voices built into your iPhone. In Settings, search for "Voices", open English, and download a female voice marked Premium. Bloom uses it once you've closed Bloom (swipe it away in the app switcher) and opened it again.

The buttons disappeared during a session.

That's quiet mode. Tap anywhere on the screen to bring them back.
